1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ELEVATOR PITCH
================================================================================
MANAS is an emotionally intelligent, culturally native 3D AI Digital Mental Twin
tailored for India's diverse linguistic landscape. While typical wellness apps
are text-only or mechanical English chatbots, MANAS combines:
- Real-time WebGL 3D humanoid avatar animation with emotive facial visemes and
  natural procedural body mechanics (e.g. human-like arm waving and breathing).
- 8 Indian languages (Tamil, Telugu, Hindi, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali,
  Marathi, English) with authentic colloquial vernacular fluency.
- Acoustically segregated voice synthesis where male and female companion
  voices strictly adhere to pitch thresholds (Male < 165 Hz, Female > 170 Hz)
  with dynamic speaking tones (Warm & Caring, Calm & Grounding, Gentle & Soft,
  Friendly & Casual, Encouraging & Motivating, Direct & Practical).
- Clinical-grade safety guardrails, on-device encryption, and zero-leakage
  architectural integrity.

primitives that established open-source projects solve best:

   - React state management, hooks (`useState`, `useEffect`, `useRef`), and
     Framer Motion transition primitives.

4. Sarvam AI Bulbul v3 TTS API:
   - Utilized Sarvam's cloud neural voice models (`bulbul:v3`) which provide the
     base voice synthesis for Indian languages.

   - Employed Gemini foundation models as the high-level language cognition engine.

6. SQLite & Python Standard Library:
   - Standard SQLite relational database engine, Python `asyncio`, `hashlib`,

4. WHAT IS INNOVATIVE (NOVEL ARCHITECTURE & OUR PROPRIETARY CONTRIBUTIONS)
================================================================================
What makes MANAS uniquely powerful is NOT simply calling APIs, but the complex,
novel engineering layers built on top of them:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INNOVATION 1: Cross-Gender Voice Disjointness & Pitch (F0) Self-Healing Engine
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The Problem: Standard TTS catalogs frequently leak opposite-gender voices across
  different regional languages (e.g. asking for a boy voice in Malayalam or Bengali
  often returns a default female voice, completely breaking user trust).
- Our Solution:
  1. Built a strict disjoint speaker registry in `voice_config.py`. Male speakers
     (`shubh`, `ratan`, `rohan`, `rehan`) and female speakers (`priya`, `ishita`,
     `ritu`, `neha`, `pooja`, `roopa`, `suhani`) are mathematically disjoint.
     Cross-gender leakage is impossible by design.
  2. Implemented an Automated Pitch (F0) Autocorrelation Test Suite
     (`test_voice_gender_f0.py`). Every synthesized audio sample is analyzed
     using normalized autocorrelation to estimate median fundamental pitch (F0).
     Male voices MUST measure < 165 Hz; female voices MUST measure > 170 Hz.
  3. Built an Acoustic Pitch-Shifting Fallback Engine (`sarvam_tts.py`). If cloud
     providers fail or lack native male timbre, audio byte buffers are resampled
     with factor 0.52 to produce rich, masculine resonance that satisfies the pitch test.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INNOVATION 2: Natural 3D Humanoid Procedural Motion Rigging (Hand Wave & Idle)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The Problem: Default WebGL avatars look like robotic rigid mannequins. Stock
  hand wave animations in Three-VRM either bend the wrong joints backwards or leave
  the fingers clenched in an awkward fist.
- Our Solution:
  1. Developed a customized procedural kinematics solver (`VRMAvatarCanvas.tsx`).
  2. Solved VRM 1.0 normalized bone orientations:
     - Right Upper Arm: Smoothly elevates `Z` rotation from resting `+1.25` to `-0.65`,
       pitching forward `X = -0.45`.
     - Right Lower Arm: Flexes inward at elbow `X = 1.25`, `Z = -0.35` bringing
       the palm up to eye level.
     - Right Hand Wrist: Oscillates horizontally with a sinusoidal trajectory
       `sin(time * 6.5) * 0.32`.
     - Procedural Finger Uncurling: During idle, humanoid fingers naturally curl
       at `0.32 rad`. When waving, our code drives individual proximal, intermediate,
       and distal phalanges of the thumb, index, middle, ring, and little fingers
       to `0.06 rad`, creating an authentic, welcoming open-palm greeting.
     - Smooth Envelope: Uses smooth cosine fade-in and fade-out over a 2.0-second
       window so the arm returns seamlessly to idle breathing without snapping.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INNOVATION 3: End-to-End Zero-Mojibake Indic Unicode Pipeline
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The Problem: Indian scripts (Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Kannada, Malayalam, Hindi,
  Marathi) frequently corrupt into garbled mojibake (e.g. `à®¸à¯...`) when streamed
  over SSE or stored in databases due to multi-byte UTF-8 boundary slicing.
- Our Solution:
  1. Iterates over raw HTTP byte chunks using incremental UTF-8 stream decoding
     (`codecs.getincrementaldecoder("utf-8")`), ensuring multi-byte Indic code points
     (which take 3-4 bytes per character) are never sliced across chunk boundaries.
  2. Enforces explicit `charset=utf-8` on all JSON and SSE HTTP response headers.
  3. Uses browser-native `TextDecoder("utf-8")` with streaming accumulation.
  4. Automated pipeline verification (`test_indic_unicode_pipeline.py`) validates
     exact byte-level round-trip preservation from LLM output through DB read.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INNOVATION 4: Contextual Dynamic Tone Conditioning & TTS Pace Synchronization
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The Problem: Most AI assistants speak in one monotone voice with a generic pace
  that doesn't match the emotional context (e.g. speaking too fast when a user is
  crying, or sounding overly clinical when someone needs warm validation).
- Our Solution:
  1. Developed 6 specialized Speaking Tones:
     - Warm & Caring (1.00x pace, deep empathy and emotional closeness)
     - Calm & Grounding (0.92x pace, centered, slows down panic/anxiety)
     - Gentle & Soft (0.92x pace, delicate care, soft-spoken comfort)
     - Friendly & Casual (1.00x pace, everyday relatable peer buddy)
     - Encouraging & Motivating (1.05x pace, uplifting hope and energy)
     - Direct & Practical (1.00x pace, concise, actionable clarity)
  2. Dual-Layer Synchronization:
     - Layer A (Cognitive): Injects a targeted tone directive into Gemini's system
       prompt so message phrasing matches the psychological intention.
     - Layer B (Acoustic): Feeds dynamic pace values directly into the TTS engine,
       so the avatar physically speaks slower or more energized based on user choice.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INNOVATION 5: Single Source of Truth Voice & Avatar Decoupling Architecture
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The Problem: In typical chat systems, avatar toggles and voice states get stuck
  in stale React closures, causing a girl avatar to speak with a boy voice or audio
  to continue playing over new selections.
- Our Solution:
  1. Built call-time profile resolution (`resolveCompanionFromProfile()`) reading
     directly from validated client storage at the exact millisecond of speech invocation.
  2. Decoupled 3D avatar visual representation from vocal gender via the
     "Voice follows companion" setting. When ON, switching Boy (Aarav) to Girl
     (Ananya) immediately toggles avatar and voice together while stopping all
     active audio. When OFF, users can pair any 3D model with any voice gender.
  3. Real-time Dev HUD (`?voicedebug=1`) displaying live telemetry badges for every
     utterance: Language, Companion, Voice Gender, Speaker, Tone, and Latency.

5. SYSTEM SUBSYSTEM DEEP-DIVES
================================================================================

A. REAL-TIME INDIC VOICE & PITCH PIPELINE
   - Supported Languages: English (en), Hindi (hi), Tamil (ta), Telugu (te),
     Kannada (kn), Malayalam (ml), Bengali (bn), Marathi (mr).
   - Speaker Catalog:
     * Girl Voices: Ananya (warm), Diya (gentle), Janani (bright/encouraging).
     * Boy Voices: Aarav (calm), Kabir (steady), Arjun (friendly).
   - Cache Architecture: Audio files cached by composite SHA-256 hash:
     `hash(text + ":" + lang + ":" + gender + ":" + speaker)`.
     This prevents cache collision between male and female speakers voicing the
     same greeting.

B. 3D WebGL HUMANOID VRM 1.0 ENGINE
   - Procedural Breathing: Subtly expands chest and rotates spine on a 3.5s sine curve.
   - Gaze Tracking & Natural Saccades: Micro-eye saccades every 2-4 seconds simulate
     human neurological presence; smooth head damping follows cursor/user touch.
   - Viseme-Accurate Lip-Sync: Audio energy from Web Audio `AnalyserNode` maps
     non-linearly to blendshape morphs (`aa`, `oh`, `ih`), moving the avatar's lips
     in lockstep with vocal cadence.

C. PRIVACY & LOCAL DATABASE ENCRYPTION
   - SQLite WAL (Write-Ahead Logging) eliminates read-write lock contention.
   - Per-user AES encrypted storage prevents unauthorized inspection of sensitive
     mental wellness chat logs on disk.
   - Anonymous session linking allows users to start instantly with zero friction
     and link mobile numbers later via SMS OTP without losing history.

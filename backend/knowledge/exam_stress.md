# Exam Stress, Academic Burnout & Cognitive Regulation

## Understanding Academic Stress
Academic pressure often triggers acute somatic anxiety (racing heartbeat, shallow breathing, sweaty palms) and cognitive freezing (mind going blank). This is an adrenaline surge intended for physical survival, not cognitive problem-solving.

## Actionable Coping Strategies
1. **The 25-5 Pomodoro Rhythm:**
   - Work in focused 25-minute intervals followed by a strict 5-minute break away from screens (stretching, water, looking out the window). After 4 cycles, take a longer 20-30 minute break.
   - Prevents mental exhaustion and preserves executive function.
2. **Combating "Mind Going Blank" During an Exam:**
   - Recognize that the memory is not erased; the stress response has temporarily restricted access to the prefrontal cortex.
   - Drop the pen, sit upright, take two physiological sighs (double inhale through nose, long exhale through mouth).
   - Start with an easy question or scribble familiar keywords on scratch paper to reactivate associative neural pathways.
3. **The "Brain Dump" Before Bed or Study:**
   - Write down every lingering task, assignment, or fear on a sheet of paper before starting study or going to bed.
   - Externalizing thoughts frees working memory and reduces intrusive cognitive loops.
4. **Separating Self-Worth from Performance:**
   - Grades evaluate mastery of specific curriculum material under timed conditions; they do not quantify human intelligence, capability, kindness, or future potential.

# Verified Crisis & Mental Health Helplines (India National & Regional)

## 24/7 Immediate Emergency Helplines
1. **Tele-MANAS (Government of India Ministry of Health & Family Welfare):**
   - **Toll-Free Numbers:** `14416` or `1800 891 4416`
   - **Availability:** 24 Hours a day, 7 Days a week, 365 Days a year
   - **Languages:** English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, Bengali, Marathi, and 12+ regional languages
   - **Services:** Free, confidential crisis intervention, mental health counseling, psychiatric consultation referrals.
2. **KIRAN Mental Health Rehabilitation Helpline (Govt of India):**
   - **Toll-Free Number:** `1800-599-0019`
   - **Availability:** 24/7
   - **Services:** Early screening, psychological first-aid, stress and anxiety support in 13 languages.
3. **Childline India (For Minors under 18 years):**
   - **Toll-Free Number:** `1098`
   - **Availability:** 24/7 Emergency outreach and support for children and teenagers facing crisis, neglect, or distress.

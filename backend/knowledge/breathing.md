# Breathing Techniques for Anxiety & Panic

## 1. 4-7-8 Relaxing Breath (Parasympathetic Reset)
- **Mechanism:** Prolongs exhalation to stimulate the vagus nerve and downregulate the autonomic nervous system from fight-or-flight to rest-and-digest.
- **Protocol:**
  1. Empty lungs completely through mouth with a gentle whoosh sound.
  2. Inhale quietly through the nose for a count of 4.
  3. Hold your breath gently for a count of 7.
  4. Exhale smoothly and completely through the mouth for a count of 8.
  5. Repeat for 4 full cycles. Stop if dizzy; return to natural breathing.

## 2. Box Breathing (Square Breathing - 4x4x4x4)
- **Mechanism:** Equalized breathing used by high-performance athletes and tactical professionals to clear mental fog and restore calm focus.
- **Protocol:**
  1. Inhale deeply through nose for 4 seconds.
  2. Hold lungs full with relaxed shoulders for 4 seconds.
  3. Exhale steadily through mouth or nose for 4 seconds.
  4. Hold lungs empty and still for 4 seconds.
  5. Complete 4-6 rounds.

## 3. Physiological Sigh (Fastest Acute Stress Reduction)
- **Mechanism:** Dual-inhalation re-inflates collapsed alveoli in the lungs and rapidly offloads excess carbon dioxide, dropping heart rate within 30 seconds.
- **Protocol:**
  1. Take a deep, brisk inhalation through the nose.
  2. At the top of the breath, take a second sharp top-up sip of air through the nose.
  3. Release a long, slow, unforced sigh through the mouth until lungs are empty.
  4. Perform 2 to 3 times in moments of acute panic or emotional overload.

# 5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding Method for Dissociation & Racing Thoughts

## Purpose
The 5-4-3-2-1 technique anchors conscious attention into the physical present, interrupting the cognitive loop of catastrophic future-thinking or past rumination by engaging all five sensory pathways.

## Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **5 Things You Can See:**
   - Look around your immediate space. Name 5 specific objects without judgment (e.g., a blue pen on the table, texture of the wood grain, reflection of light on a glass, green leaf on a plant, crack in the plaster).
2. **4 Things You Can Physically Feel:**
   - Notice tactile sensations against your body (e.g., feet firmly rooted on the cold floor, weight of your hands on your lap, texture of your cotton shirt against your arms, cool breeze on your forehead).
3. **3 Things You Can Hear:**
   - Listen outward beyond your own thoughts (e.g., hum of a ceiling fan or computer, distant traffic murmur, birds outside, steady ticking of a clock).
4. **2 Things You Can Smell:**
   - Notice subtle scents in your immediate surroundings (e.g., aroma of tea or coffee, fresh soap, scent of paper, fabric softener on clothes). If nothing is discernible, inhale deeply and imagine a favorite soothing scent like lavender or rain on warm soil (petrichor).
5. **1 Thing You Can Taste:**
   - Notice the current taste in your mouth (e.g., lingering hint of mint, cool sip of water, or simply bring attention to tongue resting comfortably on the palate).

## Coaching Tip
Remind the user: "You do not have to rush. Notice each detail slowly, feeling the ground supporting your weight underneath you."

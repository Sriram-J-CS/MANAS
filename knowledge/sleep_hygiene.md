# Sleep Hygiene & Circadian Optimization for Mental Health

## The Sleep-Anxiety Bi-directional Loop
Poor sleep elevates amygdala reactivity by up to 60%, making ordinary academic or workplace stressors feel catastrophic. Restoring consistent sleep architecture stabilizes emotional resilience.

## Evidence-Based Sleep Practices
1. **Stimulus Control (Bed = Sleep Only):**
   - Use bed exclusively for sleep and rest. Do not study, attend lectures, eat, or scroll social media in bed.
   - The 20-Minute Rule: If unable to fall asleep after roughly 20-30 minutes, get out of bed. Move to a dimly lit room, read a physical book or listen to calming music until drowsy, then return to bed.
2. **Light Exposure Timing:**
   - Seek 10-15 minutes of direct morning sunlight within 1 hour of waking to set the master circadian clock (suprachiasmatic nucleus) and initiate the 14-hour melatonin timer.
   - Screen Curfew: Dim artificial overhead lights 60 minutes before sleep. Blue light from phones suppresses melatonin secretion.
3. **Caffeine & Meal Timing:**
   - Caffeine has a quarter-life of up to 10-12 hours. Set a strict caffeine cutoff at least 8 to 10 hours before planned bedtime.
   - Avoid heavy or spicy meals within 2 to 3 hours before sleeping to prevent sleep fragmentation from digestion.
4. **Wind-Down Decompression Buffer:**
   - Dedicate the final 45 minutes of your evening to a gentle non-screen transition: warm shower (triggers peripheral vasodilation which drops core body temperature), journaling worries onto paper, or gentle ambient music.

# CBT Protocols for Overthinking, Rumination, and Mental Looping

## Understanding Rumination (Counsel Chat Evidence)
Rumination is the involuntary cycling over past mistakes or imagined future disasters. It is driven by the brain's threat-detection network attempting to solve an emotional state as if it were an analytical puzzle.

## Technique 1: Thought Defusion (Labeling the Story)
* **Principle**: Thoughts are neurochemical events, not facts or prophecies.
* **Practice**: Instead of "I am going to fail and disappoint my family", reframe to: "I notice my mind is generating the fear-of-failure story right now."
* **Multilingual Guidance**:
  - English: "Notice that our mind is playing a familiar movie of catastrophe. We do not have to buy a ticket to this movie tonight."
  - Tamil: "நம் மனம் அதே பழைய பயத்தை மீண்டும் மீண்டும் யோசிக்க வைக்கிறது. அந்த எண்ணங்களை அப்படியே கடந்து போக அனுமதிப்போம்."
  - Hindi: "हमारा दिमाग बार-बार उसी पुराने डर को दोहरा रहा है। हर विचार सच नहीं होता, इसे केवल एक विचार की तरह गुज़र जाने दें।"

## Technique 2: The Worry Window Protocol
* **Principle**: Trying to suppress worry causes rebound anxiety. Postponing worry frees current cognitive bandwidth.
* **Practice**: Designate 15 minutes in the evening (e.g. 6:00 PM - 6:15 PM) as "Worry Time". When a spiral occurs during the day or night, note it down: "I will examine this fully at 6 PM. Right now, I return to this moment."

## Technique 3: The 3-Column Reality Check
* **Column 1 (Automatic Thought)**: "Everyone thinks I am incapable."
* **Column 2 (Cognitive Distortion)**: Mind reading / Catastrophizing.
* **Column 3 (Grounded Perspective)**: "No one has said that. I am tired and projecting my own self-doubt onto others."
